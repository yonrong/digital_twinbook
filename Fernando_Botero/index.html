About This Page

This page is the bilingual entry point to an interactive knowledge graph visualization based on Marcel Paquet's 1992 book Botero: Philosophy of the Creative Act.

"Dansity"

The book reads Fernando Botero's monumental, swollen figures not as exaggeration for its own sake, but as a sustained philosophical pursuit of what Paquet calls "dansity" — the invisible, sentient density that underlies and exceeds everything visible. In this account, painting does not copy the visible world; it gives solidity and presence to what the visible necessarily lacks.

This knowledge graph maps that argument across the book's six chapters — from the founding 1956 epiphany before a sketch of a mandolin to Botero's place in Colombian cultural history — as a network of theoretical pillars, key concepts, artistic dialogues, key works, and biography.

What's in the Knowledge Graph

The interactive graph (index_en.html) contains 26 nodes, grouped into seven color-coded categories:

Core Proposition — “Dansity,” the coined term at the heart of the book
Theoretical Pillars — Solidity, Plasticity, Immanence & Transcendence, Disproportion, Style Without Concept
Key Concepts — The Visible & the Sentient, the Reflective Eye, Presentation not Representation
Artistic Dialogues — Cubism, Mantegna, Magritte, the Quattrocento, Pre-Columbian art
Key Works — Crying Woman, Still Life with Mandolin, Homage to Mantegna, Mona Lisa, Age Twelve
Chapters — the book's six-chapter structure, from “The First Steps” to “Colombia Seen Through Its Own Eyes”
Life & Legacy — Botero's biography and his place in Colombian cultural history

Clicking any node opens a detailed explanation and source citation in the sidebar.

How to Use

  • Drag nodes to rearrange the layout; scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
  • Click any node to read its detailed description in the left panel — directly connected nodes are highlighted while unrelated ones fade.
  • Click a legend entry to hide or show an entire category of nodes — focus on one sub-system at a time.
  • Use the Chapter Flow buttons (Ch. 1–6) to jump directly to a chapter node.
  • Click “Reset View” or empty space to return to the full graph and the overview panel.

費爾南多·波特羅

創作行為的哲學
馬塞爾·帕凱1992年研究之知識圖譜互動視覺化・雙語版

關於本頁

本頁是根據馬塞爾·帕凱（Marcel Paquet）1992年著作《波特羅：創作行為的哲學》（Botero: Philosophy of the Creative Act）所製作之互動式知識圖譜視覺化的雙語入口。

「感識度」Dansity

本書將費爾南多·波特羅那些雄偉、膨脹的人物形象，解讀為並非為誇張而誇張的風格手法，而是一場持續的哲學追尋——追尋帕凱所稱的「感識度」（dansity）——那潛藏於、並超越一切可見事物之下的、不可見的感性密度。在這樣的論述中，繪畫並非複製可見的世界，而是賦予可見世界中必然缺席之物以實體與臨在。

本知識圖譜將這個論證對應到全書六個章節——從1956年面對曼陀林素描的奠基性靈光一閃，到波特羅在哥倫比亞文化史上的地位——並以理論支柱、關鍵概念、藝術對話、重要作品與生平，交織成一張關係網絡。

知識圖譜的內容

互動圖譜（index_ch.html）共26個節點，分為七大色彩分類：

核心命題——「感識度」(Dansity)，全書核心的自創術語
理論支柱——堅實性、可塑性、內在性與超越性、比例失衡、無須概念的風格與溝通
關鍵概念——可見者與感性者、反思之眼、呈現而非再現
藝術對話——立體主義、曼帖那、馬格利特、文藝復興早期、前哥倫布藝術
重要作品——《哭泣的女人》、《有曼陀林的靜物》、《向曼帖那致敬》、《十二歲的蒙娜麗莎》
章節——全書六章結構，從〈最初的步伐〉到〈以自己的眼睛看見的哥倫比亞〉
生平與遺產——波特羅的生平歷程及其在哥倫比亞文化史上的地位

點擊任一節點，側邊欄將顯示該概念的詳細說明與文獻來源。

操作說明

  • 拖曳節點可重新排列版面；滾輪或手勢可放大、縮小畫面。
  • 點擊任一節點，左側面板會顯示該概念的詳細說明，並以高亮顯示其直接關聯的節點，其餘節點則淡化。
  • 點擊圖例項目可整類顯示／隱藏節點，協助聚焦於特定子系統。
  • 使用「章節脈絡」按鈕（第1～6章）可直接跳至對應章節節點。
  • 點擊「重置視圖」或畫面空白處，可回到完整圖譜與總覽面板。
