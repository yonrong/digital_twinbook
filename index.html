About This Page

This page is the bilingual entry point to an interactive knowledge graph visualization built around the thesis Dimensionality-Reduced Knowledge Graphs: A Digital Twinbook Model for Art Instinct Learning.

2³ − 1 = 7

The thesis proposes an original geometric-cognitive model: as three-dimensional beings who can only perceive the world through two-dimensional retinal projections, humans can simultaneously grasp at most 2³ − 1 = 7 of the 8 possible combinations of any “ternary structure” — the X, Y, Z axes; Peirce’s Representamen–Object–Interpretant; or concept–relation–context. The 8th combination, (1,1,1) — absolute certainty — is the permanently occluded “sun” of cognition.

This formula is cross-checked against six cross-disciplinary lines of evidence and used as a design principle for “Dimensionality-Reduced Knowledge Graphs”: projecting the vast art-historical knowledge processed by AI into a seven-dimensional structure that human cognition can grasp at a glance — enabling “Art Instinct Learning” through a prototype “Digital Twinbook.”

What's in the Knowledge Graph

The interactive graph (index_en.html) contains about 20 nodes, grouped into six color-coded categories:

Core Proposition — the 2³−1=7 dimensional-cognition formula at the heart of the thesis
Theoretical Pillars — six cross-disciplinary lines of evidence for “seven” (cube projection, Peircean semiotics, Empson, Miller, Camillo, Proverbs 9:1)
Key Concepts — Spatialization of Knowledge, Triadic Cognition, Dimensionality-Reduced Knowledge Graphs, Art Instinct Learning, Digital Twinbooks
AI & Digital Twin — Knowledge-based Digital Twin, AI as a Projection Engine, Heptabase
Chapters — the thesis’s six-chapter structure, from Introduction to Conclusion
Case Texts — Kandinsky’s Point and Line to Plane, Klee’s The Thinking Eye, Albers’s Interacting with Color

Clicking any node opens a detailed explanation and source citation in the sidebar.

How to Use

  • Drag nodes to rearrange the layout; scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
  • Click any node to read its detailed description in the left panel — directly connected nodes are highlighted while unrelated ones fade.
  • Click a legend entry to hide or show an entire category of nodes — “reduce the dimension” of the graph to focus on one sub-system at a time.
  • Use the Chapter Flow buttons (Ch.1–Ch.6) to jump directly to a chapter node.
  • Click “Reset View” or empty space to return to the full graph and the overview panel.

降維知識圖譜

數位孿生書的藝術本能學習模型
論文知識圖譜互動視覺化・雙語版

關於本頁

本頁是論文《降維知識圖譜：數位孿生書的藝術本能學習模型》互動式知識圖譜視覺化的雙語入口。

2³ − 1 = 7

本論文提出一個原創的幾何認知模型：人類作為三維生物，僅能透過二維視網膜投影理解世界，因此面對任何「三元結構」──如 X、Y、Z 三軸；皮爾斯符號學的再現體・對象・解釋體；或概念・關係・脈絡──時，能同時掌握的維度組合數恆為 2³ − 1 = 7。第 8 種組合「(1,1,1)」──絕對確定的狀態──是永遠被遮蔽、無法直視的認知「太陽」。

本論文以六條跨領域理論脈絡印證這個公式，並以此作為「降維知識圖譜」的設計原則：將 AI 處理的龐雜藝術知識，投影為人類可一眼掌握的七維結構，並透過「數位孿生書」原型實現「藝術本能學習」。

知識圖譜的內容

互動圖譜（index_ch.html）共約 20 個節點，分為六大色彩分類：

核心命題──貫穿全文的 2³−1=7 降維認知公式
理論支柱──跨領域印證「七」的六條線索（立方體幾何投影、皮爾斯三元符號學、艾普森《七種曖昧》、米勒神奇數字 7±2、卡米羅記憶劇場、箴言 9:1）
核心概念──知識空間化、三元認知、降維知識圖譜、藝術本能學習、數位孿生書
技術應用──知識型數位孿生、AI 投影引擎、Heptabase
論文章節──論文六章結構，從緒論到結論
案例文本──康定斯基《點線面》、保羅・克利《思考之眼》、約瑟夫・阿爾伯斯《色彩互動學》

點擊任一節點，側邊欄將顯示該概念的詳細說明與文獻來源。

操作說明

  • 拖曳節點可重新排列版面；滾輪或手勢可放大、縮小畫面。
  • 點擊任一節點，左側面板會顯示該概念的詳細說明，並以高亮顯示其直接關聯的節點，其餘節點則淡化。
  • 點擊圖例項目可整類顯示／隱藏節點──即「降維」操作，協助聚焦於特定子系統。
  • 使用「章節脈絡」按鈕（第 1～6 章）可直接跳至對應章節節點。
  • 點擊「重置視圖」或畫面空白處，可回到完整圖譜與總覽面板。
