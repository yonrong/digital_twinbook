Fernando Botero in the Classroom

An Art-Instinct Knowledge Graph for Educators
Interactive Knowledge Graph Based on MOLAA's 2023 Educator's Packet · Bilingual Edition

About This Page

This page is the bilingual entry point to an interactive knowledge graph built from the Museum of Latin American Art's (MOLAA) 2023 Educator's Packet for the traveling exhibition Fernando Botero: The Master (a Smithsonian Affiliate exhibition).

AI × AI

The graph is built around a deliberate pun. AI stands for Artificial Intelligence — the technology used to organize this teaching resource — and also for Art Instinct — the intuitive sense of form, proportion, symbolism and feeling that looking at, talking about, and making art can cultivate in every student.

It maps Botero's signature style (Boterismo), the art vocabulary needed to discuss it, Visual Thinking Strategies and gallery games for guided looking, hands-on classroom activities, the featured artworks that anchor each activity, and Botero's 91-year life story — all woven into a single connected network.

What's in the Knowledge Graph

The interactive graph (index_en.html) contains 31 nodes, grouped into six color-coded categories:

Boterismo · AI × AI — the signature style at the heart of the graph, and the bridge between Artificial Intelligence and Art Instinct
Art Vocabulary — 11 key terms from the packet, from Figurative Art and Symbolism to Bronze Cast Sculpture and Vallenato
Looking & Teaching Methods — the three VTS questions, Close Looking, Gallery Games, and the Flash Memory game
Classroom Activities — the wire & paper-mâché sculpture lesson, family portraits, and the artist-timeline activity
Featured Artworks — Family in the Plantation, Still Life with Watermelon, Horse / Caballo, La Familia, Reclining Woman, Still Life with Mandolin
Life & Legacy — Botero's biography, from Medellín in 1932 to MOLAA's The Master in 2022

Clicking any node opens a detailed explanation and page citation in the sidebar.

How to Use

  • Drag nodes to rearrange the layout; scroll or pinch to zoom in and out.
  • Click any node to read its detailed description in the left panel — directly connected nodes are highlighted while unrelated ones fade.
  • Click a legend entry to hide or show an entire category of nodes — focus on one part of the lesson at a time.
  • Use the Teaching Flow buttons (1–6: Style · Observe · Sculpt · Portrait · Timeline · Legacy) to jump directly to a key lesson node.
  • Click “Reset View” or empty space to return to the full graph and the overview panel.

費爾南多·波特羅的課堂

為教育者打造的藝術直覺知識圖譜
取材自MOLAA 2023年教育者手冊之互動知識圖譜・雙語版

關於本頁

本頁是依據拉丁美洲藝術博物館（Museum of Latin American Art, MOLAA）為巡迴展覽《費爾南多·波特羅：大師》Fernando Botero: The Master（史密森尼附屬機構 Smithsonian Affiliate 展覽）所製作的2023年教育者手冊，建立之互動知識圖譜的雙語入口。

AI × AI

本圖譜建立於一個刻意設計的雙關語之上：AI 既是 Artificial Intelligence（人工智能）——用來組織這份教學資源的技術；也是 Art Instinct（藝術直覺）——觀看、討論與創作藝術，能在每一位學生身上培養出的、對形體、比例、象徵與情感的直觀感受。

圖譜將波特羅的招牌風格（波特羅主義）、描述它所需的藝術詞彙、用於引導觀看的視覺思考策略與展廳遊戲、動手實作的課堂活動、支撐每項活動的重點作品，以及波特羅91年的人生故事，全部交織成一張相互連結的關係網絡。

知識圖譜的內容

互動圖譜（index_ch.html）共31個節點，分為六大色彩分類：

波特羅主義 · AI × AI ——貫穿全圖的核心風格，也是人工智能與藝術直覺之間的橋樑
藝術詞彙——手冊中的11個關鍵詞彙，從具象藝術、象徵主義到青銅鑄造雕塑與瓦列納托音樂
觀看與教學方法——VTS三個核心提問、細緻觀看、展廳遊戲，以及快閃記憶遊戲
課堂活動——鐵絲與紙漿雕塑課程、家族肖像活動，以及藝術家年表活動
重點作品——《種植園裡的家族》、《有西瓜的靜物》、《馬／Caballo》、《家族》、《臥女》、《有曼陀林的靜物》
生平與遺產——波特羅的生平，從1932年的麥德林到2022年MOLAA的《大師》展

點擊任一節點，側邊欄將顯示該主題的詳細說明與頁碼出處。

操作說明

  • 拖曳節點可重新排列版面；滾輪或手勢可放大、縮小畫面。
  • 點擊任一節點，左側面板會顯示該主題的詳細說明，並以高亮顯示其直接關聯的節點，其餘節點則淡化。
  • 點擊圖例項目可整類顯示／隱藏節點，協助一次聚焦於課程的某個部分。
  • 使用「教學流程」按鈕（1～6：風格・觀察・雕塑・肖像・年表・傳承）可直接跳至對應的關鍵教學節點。
  • 點擊「重置視圖」或畫面空白處，可回到完整圖譜與總覽面板。
