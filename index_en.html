How to Use

• Drag nodes to rearrange the layout; scroll or pinch to zoom.
• Click any node to see its detailed description on the right, with directly connected nodes highlighted.
• Click a legend item to "reduce the dimension" of the graph — hiding or showing an entire category of nodes to focus on a sub-system.
• Click "Reset View" to restore the full graph.

Tip: click a node for details; click empty space or "Reset View" to return to the overview.
